Innovation project 01Mechanical design

Solar-panel cleaning robot

A concept for a climbing robot that reaches high solar panels and cleans their surface. My contribution was the mechanical representation of the idea.

  • Part and assembly modelling in SolidWorks.
  • Explored how a robot could grip, climb and carry a cleaning mechanism.

Simulation study 02Robotics / ROS 2

Three-mode mobile robot

A small-car simulation with a LiDAR sensor. The same robot can switch between autonomous driving, click-to-go navigation and keyboard control.

  • Modelled and tested the robot in Ubuntu with ROS 2 and Gazebo.
  • Used simulation to compare different ways of commanding motion.
